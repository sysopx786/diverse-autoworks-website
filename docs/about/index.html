    History

    Established in 2007.

    Diverse Autoworks Inc. is a family run business in which our goal and main priority is to deliver complete satisfaction for every customer through honest and thorough workmanship, while still offering knowledgeable and friendly service at affordable rates. We are an auto repair shop specializing in both domestic and foreign vehicles. We offer State Inspection and Emissions services as well as tags and notary services. We stand behind our work, including A/C service, alignments, batteries, brakes and rotors, engine analysis and tune-ups, rear axle bearings and seals, shock absorbers and struts, transmission maintenance, and much more.
