      Fleet maintenance for work use

      Towing, hauling, idling on job sites, short trips and stop-and-go routes all count as severe service in most owner's manuals. Most fleet vehicles fall into that group, so they need service sooner than the same model driven gently.

      Many work trucks set their own oil change timing. Here's how the common ones work:

      MakeWhat sets the oil changeFirm limit
      Ford (F-Series, Transit)Intelligent Oil-Life Monitor1 year or 10,000 miles
      Chevrolet and GMCEngine Oil Life SystemAt least once a year
      Ram (2025 Ram 1500)Oil change indicator10,000 miles, 12 months or 350 hours of engine run time

      The 350-hour limit catches trucks that idle a lot: they can reach it long before 10,000 miles. We reset each monitor after the oil change, so the next reminder is accurate. Your owner's manual lists the severe-service schedule for each vehicle.

      Keep one vehicle down at a time

      Plan maintenance and inspections so only one vehicle is out of service at a time. A short list of what's due on each vehicle makes that easy, and we'll tell you what's coming up when each one is in.

      Pennsylvania inspections for fleet vehicles

      Every vehicle needs Pennsylvania's annual safety inspection. Whether it also needs an emissions test depends on its fuel, its weight rating and where it's registered.

      Vehicle registered in Chester or Montgomery CountySafety inspectionEmissions test
      Gas or hybrid, gross vehicle weight rating (GVWR) of 9,000 lb or less: most cars, SUVs and half-ton pickupsYesYes
      Gas, GVWR over 9,000 lb: many 3/4- and 1-ton pickups and vansYesNo
      DieselYesNo

      You'll find the GVWR on the label inside the driver's door. We do the safety inspection and emissions test at the same visit. See state inspections.

      Fleet repair FAQs

      What business vehicles do you service?

      Gas and diesel pickups, heavy-duty pickups, chassis cabs, cargo vans, box trucks, SUVs, sedans and minivans, including the Ford F-150, Super Duty and Transit, Chevrolet Silverado and Express, GMC Sierra, Ram 1500 through 5500, Ram ProMaster and Mercedes-Benz Sprinter. For hybrids, we handle inspections, tires, brakes and suspension.

      Can you do Pennsylvania inspections for all our vehicles?

      Yes. We do the annual safety inspection and, for vehicles that need it, the emissions test at the same visit.

      Do our work trucks need an emissions test?

      Gas and hybrid vehicles with a GVWR of 9,000 lb or less that are registered in Chester or Montgomery County need one. Diesel vehicles and gas vehicles over 9,000 lb GVWR need only the safety inspection. The GVWR is on the label inside the driver's door.

      Can you handle tags, titles and plates for new fleet vehicles?

      Yes. Diverse Autoworks is a PennDOT authorized agent, so we handle tag and title work and issue plates. Our notary is available Monday through Friday, 8 a.m. to 3 p.m.

      What should I include in a fleet inquiry?

      The number of vehicles, the year, make and model of each, the work needed, your preferred scheduling and a contact person for your business.

      Will servicing fleet vehicles at an independent shop void the warranty?

      No. Under federal law, a manufacturer or dealer can't deny warranty coverage just because someone other than the dealer did your routine maintenance. Keep your receipts from every service.
