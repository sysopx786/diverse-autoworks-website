    Acura FAQs

    What does the A or B code on my Acura mean?

    A means an engine oil change. B means an oil and filter change plus inspections. Numbers after the letter add items, such as 1 for a tire rotation.

    When does the Acura Maintenance Minder show a code?

    When oil life reaches 15%, the dash shows that service is due soon, with a code.

    Does my Acura have a timing belt?

    Some Acura V6 engines use a timing belt. Code 4 shows when it's due on equipped models. We do timing belt service.

    Is an Acura a Honda?

    Acura is Honda's luxury brand, and both use the same Maintenance Minder system.

    Will servicing my Acura at an independent shop void the warranty?

    No. Under federal law, a manufacturer or dealer can't deny warranty coverage just because someone other than the dealer did your routine maintenance. Keep your receipts from every service.
