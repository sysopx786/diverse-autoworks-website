    Lexus service in Phoenixville, PA

    Most current Lexus models use full synthetic oil and need an oil change every 10,000 miles or 12 months, with a tire and inspection visit in between.

    You don't need the dealer for routine service. Under federal law, a manufacturer or dealer can't deny your warranty coverage just because an independent shop did your routine maintenance. Keep your receipts from every service.

    After complimentary maintenance ends

    Lexus Complimentary Maintenance covers the first two scheduled visits, at 5,000 miles or 6 months and 10,000 miles or 12 months. After that, you can keep your Lexus on the same schedule with us.

    Pennsylvania state inspection

    Pennsylvania requires an annual safety inspection. Vehicles registered in Chester and Montgomery counties also need an emissions test, done at the same visit. See state inspections.

    RepairPal rates Lexus 4.0 out of 5 for reliability, 7th out of 32 car brands. Lexus vehicles average 0.5 unscheduled shop visits a year, and 8% of repairs are severe.

    Lexus FAQs

    How often does a Lexus need an oil change?

    Most current models with full synthetic oil need one every 10,000 miles or 12 months. Severe use shortens that to 5,000 miles or 6 months.

    What happens at the 5,000-mile Lexus service?

    Tire rotation, a brake inspection and fluid level checks.

    What does Lexus Complimentary Maintenance cover?

    The first two scheduled visits, at 5,000 miles or 6 months and 10,000 miles or 12 months.

    Is a Lexus a Toyota?

    Lexus is Toyota's luxury brand. Many Lexus models share engines and platforms with Toyotas.

    Will servicing my Lexus at an independent shop void the warranty?

    No. Under federal law, a manufacturer or dealer can't deny warranty coverage just because someone other than the dealer did your routine maintenance. Keep your receipts from every service.
