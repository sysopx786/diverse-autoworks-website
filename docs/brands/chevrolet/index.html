    Chevrolet service in Phoenixville, PA

    Most Chevrolets use the Engine Oil Life System. Chevrolet says it factors in speed, engine temperature, driving style and local weather to decide when your oil needs changing.

    You don't need the dealer for routine service. Under federal law, a manufacturer or dealer can't deny your warranty coverage just because an independent shop did your routine maintenance. Keep your receipts from every service.

    Chevrolet maintenance schedule: the Oil Life System

    What Chevrolet saysWhat it means for you
    The Oil Life System tells you when an oil change is dueChange the oil when the reminder appears
    Oil and filter must be changed at least once a yearEven with low mileage, change it yearly
    The system must be reset after each changeWithout a reset, the next reminder is wrong

    Under easy driving, the system may not call for service for more than a year, which is why the once-a-year rule matters. Your owner's manual lists the tire rotation and other intervals for your model.

    Chevrolet FAQs

    How often does a Chevy need an oil change?

    When the Oil Life System says so, and at least once a year, according to Chevrolet.

    My Chevy's oil life still shows a high percentage after a year. Do I still need an oil change?

    Yes. Chevrolet says the oil and filter must be changed at least once a year, even if the system hasn't called for it.

    What does the Chevrolet Oil Life System look at?

    Chevrolet says it factors in speed, engine temperature, driving style and local weather.

    Do you work on Silverado and Colorado pickups?

    Yes. We service Chevy pickups, including brakes, suspension, differential repair and transmission maintenance.

    Do you repair Chevy work trucks and fleet vehicles?

    Yes. We offer fleet repairs for businesses running Chevrolet trucks and vans.
