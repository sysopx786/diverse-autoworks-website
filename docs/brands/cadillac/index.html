    Cadillac service in Phoenixville, PA

    Cadillacs use GM's oil life monitoring system to set the oil change schedule, with a firm 12-month limit.

    You don't need the dealer for routine service. Under federal law, a manufacturer or dealer can't deny your warranty coverage just because an independent shop did your routine maintenance. Keep your receipts from every service.

    Oil and filterWhen the oil life monitor calls for it; never more than 12 months
    Tire rotationAbout every 7,500 miles

    Average cost of Cadillac maintenance

    Cadillacs average about $783 a year in repair and maintenance costs, according to RepairPal, compared with an industry average of about $652. Larger models like the Escalade usually cost more to maintain than the smaller sedans.

    How reliable are Cadillacs?

    RepairPal rates Cadillac 3.0 out of 5 for reliability, 26th out of 32 car brands. Cadillacs average 0.4 unscheduled shop visits a year, and 17% of repairs are severe.

    Cadillac FAQs

    How often does a Cadillac need an oil change?

    When the oil life monitor calls for it, and never more than 12 months apart.

    How often should Cadillac tires be rotated?

    About every 7,500 miles, according to Cadillac's maintenance guidance.

    Do you service the Cadillac Escalade?

    Yes. We service the Escalade and Cadillac's other SUVs and sedans.

    Why do Cadillacs cost more to maintain than Chevrolets?

    RepairPal puts Cadillac at about $783 a year and Chevrolet at about $649. Larger engines, more equipment and luxury parts can add cost.

    Will servicing my Cadillac at an independent shop void the warranty?

    No. Under federal law, a manufacturer or dealer can't deny warranty coverage just because someone other than the dealer did your routine maintenance. Keep your receipts from every service.
