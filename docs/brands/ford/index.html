    Ford maintenance schedule: the Intelligent Oil-Life Monitor

    Most newer Fords use the Intelligent Oil-Life Monitor. It calculates oil life from how you drive and shows a message when an oil change is due.

    SituationFord's guidance
    Normal drivingOil change when the message appears, up to 1 year or 10,000 miles
    After the message appearsChange the oil within 2 weeks or 500 miles
    Towing, heavy idling, short trips, cold weatherThe monitor shortens the interval on its own
    Monitor reset incorrectly or not workingFall back to every 6 months or 5,000 miles

    The monitor must be reset

    The monitor has to be reset after every oil change so the next reminder is accurate.

    Ford FAQs

    How often does my Ford need an oil change?

    When the Intelligent Oil-Life Monitor says so. Under normal driving, Ford's maximum is 1 year or 10,000 miles, whichever comes first.

    What should I do when the oil change message appears?

    Ford says to change the oil within 2 weeks or 500 miles of the message appearing.

    What happens if the oil-life monitor isn't reset?

    Ford says the monitor falls back to a 6-month or 5,000-mile interval if it was reset early, wasn't reset at an oil change, or isn't working.

    Do you work on Ford work trucks and fleet vehicles?

    Yes. We service Ford trucks and vans, and we offer fleet repairs for businesses.

    Will servicing my Ford at an independent shop void the warranty?

    No. Under federal law, a manufacturer or dealer can't deny warranty coverage just because someone other than the dealer did your routine maintenance. Keep your receipts from every service.
