    PennDOT authorized agent in Phoenixville, PA

    Authorized agents are private businesses under contract to PennDOT to complete title and registration applications. PennDOT's title applications, Form MV-4ST for a Pennsylvania title and Form MV-1 for a vehicle titled in another state, aren't available online. An agent completes them with you.

    At Diverse Autoworks you can get the title signed in front of our notary, file the title and registration, get your plates, and pass the state inspection, all at one shop.

    • Titles and plates for business and fleet vehicles

    Buying or selling a car privately

    In Pennsylvania, the seller's signature on the title must be notarized or verified, and the buyer has 20 days from the sale to apply for the new title. Do the signing and the title application in one visit.

    WhoWhat to bring
    SellerThe original title in your name, unsigned; current photo ID; the lien release if the vehicle had a loan
    BuyerPhoto ID that matches the name on the application; proof of Pennsylvania insurance for the vehicle

    What happens to the plate

    After the title transfers, the seller removes the plate and keeps it for another vehicle or returns it to PennDOT. Never give the plate to the buyer. If the buyer already has a Pennsylvania plate, we can move it to the vehicle they're buying.

    Inspection after the sale

    PennDOT requires the vehicle to be inspected within 10 days of the sale unless it shows a valid inspection sticker. We do Pennsylvania safety inspections and emissions tests here. See state inspections.

    For signing rules, joint owners, gifts between family members and out-of-state titles, see our notary guide.

    Moving to Pennsylvania with your vehicle

    New residents must apply for a Pennsylvania title and registration within 20 days of moving here. Bring:

    • Your out-of-state title, issued in your name
    • Proof of Pennsylvania insurance, such as an insurance ID card or declaration page
    • Photo ID
    • A VIN tracing, or a VIN verification by a certified inspection mechanic or notary

    If a lender holds your title, contact them first: many out-of-state lenders won't release a title until the loan is paid off.

    Once the vehicle is registered in Pennsylvania, it must pass a safety inspection within 10 days. Most gas and hybrid vehicles registered in Chester and Montgomery counties also need the emissions test, and we do both at the same visit.

    Tag and title FAQs

    Are you a PennDOT authorized agent?

    Yes. Diverse Autoworks is a PennDOT authorized agent. We handle tag and title work and issue license plates at 1415 Pawlings Road in Phoenixville.

    I bought a car from a private seller. What do I need to transfer the title?

    The seller's signed and notarized title, photo ID that matches the name on the application, and proof of Pennsylvania insurance for the vehicle. If the seller hasn't signed yet, come in together and our notary can witness the signature. Sales tax and PennDOT fees are paid when we file the application.

    How long do I have to title a vehicle I bought?

    Pennsylvania law gives the buyer 20 days from the sale to apply for a new title. The vehicle also needs a safety inspection within 10 days of the sale, unless it has a valid sticker.

    Can I move my license plate to the car I just bought?

    Yes. If you have a Pennsylvania plate from a vehicle you sold or traded, we can transfer it to the vehicle you bought.

    I just moved to Pennsylvania. How do I register my car?

    Apply within 20 days of moving. Bring your out-of-state title in your name, proof of Pennsylvania insurance, photo ID and a VIN tracing or verification. We complete Form MV-1 with you. After registration, the vehicle needs a Pennsylvania safety inspection within 10 days, and we do that here too.

    How much does a title transfer cost?

    PennDOT lists a $72 title fee and $48 a year to register a passenger vehicle, plus 6% sales tax on the purchase price or market value for Chester County residents. Agents may add a service fee. Call us for the total on your transaction.

    What should the seller do with the license plate?

    Remove it after the title transfers. Keep it to move to another vehicle you own, or return it to PennDOT. Don't give the plate to the buyer.

    Is tag and title work the same as your notary service?

    No. The notary witnesses signatures on the title and other documents, Monday through Friday, 8 a.m. to 3 p.m. Tag and title work is the PennDOT application, registration and plates. Most title transfers need both, and you can do both here in one visit.
