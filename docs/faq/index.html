    Motorcycle inspections

    Do you perform motorcycle state inspections?

    Yes. Motorcycle state inspections are included in our inspection services. Call the shop to arrange your visit.

    What does a Pennsylvania motorcycle safety inspection cover?

    PennDOT lists items including brakes, steering, suspension, tires and wheels, lights and electrical systems, mirrors, fuel and exhaust systems, the horn, and the body and chassis. An inspection evaluates the required safety components; it is not the same as a complete engine-service appointment.

    Do you offer motorcycle repairs as well as inspections?

    Our published motorcycle offering is state inspection. Please call with your motorcycle's year, make, model, and repair needs to confirm whether we can help with anything beyond the inspection.

    Tires, tire repairs & wheel alignments

    What tire services do you offer?

    Our published services include tires, tire repairs, and wheel alignments. Contact the shop to discuss the work your vehicle needs.

    How can I tell whether I might need an alignment?

    Pulling to one side, an off-center steering wheel, or uneven tire wear can indicate an alignment concern. Other tire or suspension issues can cause similar symptoms, so the cause needs to be assessed rather than assumed.

    Steering & suspension

    What steering and suspension work do you offer?

    Our advertised service range includes steering parts, suspension parts, shock absorbers, and struts. Call with your vehicle details to discuss the work needed.

    Is a wheel alignment the same as suspension repair?

    No. Alignment adjusts suspension angles to the vehicle's specifications. Repairing worn or damaged suspension parts is a separate task. The work needed depends on the condition of your vehicle.

    Can pulling or vibration have more than one cause?

    Yes. Tire pressure, tire condition, wheel balance, alignment, and suspension problems can produce overlapping symptoms. Describe when the problem occurs when you contact the shop.
