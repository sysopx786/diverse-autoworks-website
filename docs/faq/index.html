    Motorcycle inspections

    Year-round motorcycle inspection guide

    Do you perform motorcycle state inspections?

    Yes. Motorcycle state inspections are included in our inspection services. Call the shop to arrange your visit.

    What does a Pennsylvania motorcycle safety inspection cover?

    PennDOT lists items including brakes, steering, suspension, tires and wheels, lights and electrical systems, mirrors, fuel and exhaust systems, the horn, and the body and chassis. An inspection evaluates the required safety components; it is not the same as a complete engine-service appointment.

    Do you offer motorcycle repairs as well as inspections?

    Engine analysis, tune-ups & fuel systems

    Do you offer engine diagnostics and tune-ups?

    Yes. We offer engine analysis, engine tune-ups, fuel-injector cleaning, and engine repair. Call 610-650-0316 and describe the problem so we can book the right appointment.

    What should I tell you when requesting engine analysis?

    Tell us your vehicle's year, make, and model, what you have noticed, when it happens, and whether a warning light is showing. Mention any recent work as well. This information helps explain your request; it does not replace an assessment of the vehicle.

    What engine problems do you repair?

    Ignition and fuel: spark plugs, ignition coils, fuel injectors, fuel pump, fuel filter, fuel lines, fuel pressure regulator, throttle body, and mass airflow sensor. Air and emissions: air filter, vacuum leaks, PCV valve, intake manifold gasket, oxygen sensors, catalytic converter, EGR valve, and EVAP leaks. Sensors and electronics: engine control module, coolant temperature, manifold pressure, throttle position, and knock sensors, and check-engine diagnosis. Cooling: water pump, thermostat, radiator, hoses, cooling fan, coolant leaks, coolant flush, and head gasket. Oil and seals: oil pump, oil pressure sensor, valve cover, oil pan, rear main and front crankshaft seals, and oil consumption. Timing and valves: timing belt or chain, tensioner, guides, valve adjustment, valve seals, rocker arms, lifters, and camshaft and crankshaft seals. Belts and accessories: serpentine and accessory belts, tensioners, idler pulleys, alternator, and A/C compressor. Internal: compression loss, piston rings, cylinder head cracks, cylinder head and block repair, rod and main bearing knock, and engine rebuild or replacement. Mounts: motor mounts.

    What symptoms should I call about?

    Misfires, rough idle, stalling, hesitation, loss of power, poor fuel economy, knocking or pinging, overheating, blue, white, or black exhaust smoke, ticking, knocking, whining, or tapping noises, and a check-engine light. Several causes can produce the same symptom, so the shop needs to diagnose it.

    Do you rebuild or replace engines?

    Yes. Engine rebuild and replacement are part of our engine repair work. Call 610-650-0316 to discuss the work your vehicle needs.
