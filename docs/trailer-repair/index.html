    Trailer Repair FAQs

    What trailer makes and models do you work on?

    Most makes and models weighing 10,000 pounds or less. Tell us about your trailer to confirm the service.

    Does the 10,000-pound limit apply to my truck?

    No. The limit refers to the trailer's weight, not the towing vehicle. Please also provide the manufacturer's weight rating when asking about service.

    Can you repair an enclosed cargo trailer?

    Contact us with its make, model, weight and the specific problem. We will confirm what we can repair.

    Do you work on landscaping or equipment trailers?

    We accept inquiries for these types within our weight limit. Individual repairs are confirmed after we review the details.

    Can you service a boat or camper trailer?

    Ask us about the trailer's roadgoing components. We have not confirmed repair of boats, RV appliances or living quarters.

    Do you perform Pennsylvania trailer inspections?

    Trailers are listed among our Pennsylvania safety inspection services. Call with the trailer classification and weight to confirm eligibility and availability.

    Are online requests confirmed appointments?

    No. Our staff will review your request and contact you to confirm availability.
